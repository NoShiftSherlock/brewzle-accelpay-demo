How Brewzle drops work

Curated drops, member pre-sale

Instead of releasing one barrel at a time, Brewzle drops are curated collections of barrel picks gathered throughout the year, from small regional craft distilleries to the well known Kentucky houses.

Brewzle Club members get priority access in a pre-sale window for every barrel pick. After that, remaining bottles open to the public.

The Brewzle community

Picked with the community

From Brewzle Fest to distillery takeovers, picks are often chosen live with Club members and fans.

With the AccelPay app, every bottle sale lands in the same Shopify admin as your merch, so you see the whole customer in one place.

How this works

This is Brewzle's own Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed. Barrel picks sit next to the merch, in the native Shopify cart and checkout. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

01Shopify cartShoppers add to cart on this site, in the native Shopify cart. No third-party cart and no redirect.
02Shopify checkoutThe one-page Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and card payments by AccelPay.
03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer that sells and ships the order. Retailers set the final retail price.
04Your customer dataEvery order and customer record lands in Brewzle's Shopify admin, next to your merch and Club members. One store, one customer list.
For the Brewzle team

One store for picks, merch and the Club

Today the bottles sell on a separate bottle-shop site. With the AccelPay Shopify app, barrel picks sell on brewzle.com in the same theme, cart and checkout as your merch. AccelPay takes over payments, taxes and order routing to a licensed retailer, and the customer data stays in your Shopify admin.

  • Ships to 40+ states through 3,500+ licensed retail locations
  • Club pre-sale windows work like any Shopify drop: tag members, open the collection to them first
  • Instant delivery in about 30 minutes through Gopuff, plus DoorDash from local retailers, where a retailer stocks the bottle
  • Live in about one week from kickoff, with no per-order charge to the brand
  • A ReserveBar.com listing comes with it

Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.

Reordering

Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
